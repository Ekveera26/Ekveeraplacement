Curriculum Roadmap

क्या-क्या सिखाया जाएगा

Beginner से लेकर Interview-ready तक, 15 दिनों का पूरा practical training module।

🗓️ Days 1–3: Call Center Basics & BPO Overview

Call Center का परिचय, Inbound/Outbound Calling, Process Workflow, Professional Greeting और BPO Etiquettes।

🗓️ Days 4–7: Communication & Soft Skills

Active Listening skills, Voice Modulation, Pronunciation, Probing Techniques, और Pitch Perfecting.

🗓️ Days 8–11: Customer Handling & Scenario Practical

Customer Empathy, Handling Difficult Customers, Call Hold/Transfer Protocols, Dead Air & Objection Handling.

🗓️ Days 12–13: Performance KPIs & CRM Skills

Call Disposition, Wrap-up Time, Average Handling Time (AHT), First Call Resolution (FCR) समझना और CRM Tools।

🗓️ Days 14–15: Live Mock Calls & Interview Preparation

Live Scenario Practice, Live Call Audits, Interview Tips, HR Question Prep, Personality Development & Guidance。
